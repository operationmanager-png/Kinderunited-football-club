Our motto

Our philosophy is simple

We specialise in sports education and infrastructure management. Every child follows a clear roadmap from beginner to advanced level, guided by our coaching programme and regular assessments.

BeginnerFundamentals and fun
IntermediateTechnique and game sense
AdvancedTactics and competition
ElitePathway to elite football

Why parents choose Kinder United

Everything a young player needs to improve, in one place.

Professional licensed coaches

Qualified coaches and dedicated managers run every session.

A full-year curriculum

A structured plan for the whole season, session by session.

Six training days a week

Monday to Saturday, with Saturday's session played as a practice match so players put the week's lesson into a real game.

Tournament football

Our U-12, U-15 and U-17 teams play tournaments across Baroda through the season.

Workshops with the experts

Every three months, well-known sportspersons and sports physios work with our players and parents.

Progress you can see

Regular reports, a parents' meeting every quarter, and the academy app for schedules and assessments.

Our home ground

Sama Sports Park, Vadodara. The full-size court splits into two 5-a-side courts with a sliding net, so two groups can train at once.

One consistent home ground, the same coaches every session, and professional equipment on site.

The club kit

Ground opening weekend

A two-day youth tournament to open Sama Sports Park, with each age group on its own pitch so all three run at the same time.

U-125-a-side ground B8 teams, 2 × 10 min matches
U-155-a-side ground A8 teams, 2 × 12 min matches
U-177-a-side ground8 teams, 2 × 15 min matches

  1. Saturday: group stage

    All group matches in a morning session and an evening session, avoiding the midday heat.

  2. Sunday: finals and launch

    Semi-finals in the morning, a free Kinder United demo session for young children, finals in the evening and the prize ceremony with the academy launch.
